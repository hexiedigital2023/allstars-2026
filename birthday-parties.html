Good to know before you book

Booking & deposits

A 50% deposit is required upon booking to secure your reservation. If no deposit is placed within 24 hours of booking, the event will be cancelled. Guest count, date, time and food/drink selections are confirmed a minimum of 72 hours before your event.

Cancellations

With more than 14 days' notice, 50% of the deposit is retained and the remaining balance refunded. With less than 14 days' notice, no refund is offered. The remaining balance is due in full on the day of your event.

Decorations & food safety

We encourage you to customize your child's party — let us know how we can help. For guest safety and site cleanliness, no confetti, confetti cannons, party poppers, glow sticks, piñatas, glitter, sparklers or latex balloons are allowed on site; please use foil balloons instead. If providing treat/loot bags, please distribute these at pickup time. No outside food or drinks are permitted, except a special cake or dessert with any party package. Allstars is a nut-free facility.

On the day

Everyone, including adults, must wear socks (available for purchase in guest services if forgotten). Parents/caregivers are responsible for supervising their children throughout the party. We enforce a no-bullying policy, and admission is charged for each visit — re-entry requires another entry fee.
