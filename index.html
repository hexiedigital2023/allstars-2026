Welcome To Allstars Indoor Playland

where every child is a star, and every star shines!

Allstars Indoor Playland is Canada's largest indoor playground, offering a unique venue for child development, both physical and social. We've been serving Edmonton families since 2016 — a safe place for parents and children with the focus on adventure and fun. Watch your children climb, slide, jump and most importantly, smile.
