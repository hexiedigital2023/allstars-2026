What's inside

Over 28,000 Sqft to Explore

With the largest dedicated toddler area in Edmonton, the largest climbing structure in Canada, a 115' zipline, multiple areas to host the perfect party, trampolines, arcade games, and our Kid's Corner, we're first on your list of indoor playland destinations.

10 party rooms!

Everything you need to host your child's memories and special occasions. In addition to any special requests, our party hosts will be happy to assist in the set-up and clean-up of your function. We ensure that your Allstar IS the star of the show!

Largest Playstructure

Your child will enjoy running, climbing and sliding with you through the largest playstructure in Canada. Equipped with multiple tunnels, slides, and more to keep them busy for hours!

Special Toddlers Space

The largest dedicated toddler zone in Edmonton, lined with soft-floor covering and filled with soft and sensory toys. Your toddler will hone their motor skills, cultivate their social interactions and learn through play.

RopeLand Crochet Playground

A new addition to our facility. It encourages children crossing various levels, tunnels, and playground areas while looking for the right path — learning balance by jumping on upper levels, swinging on the balls, climbing, or just rolling in the soft mesh.

115' Zipline

Watch your child fly through the air on our 115' zipline. You must be 50 lbs to 100 lbs to ride — kids only!

Arcade Games

With multiple token packages available, everyone in the family can enjoy our state-of-the-art arcade games. With smart card technology, you no longer need to carry tokens or tickets from game to game — swipe the card, play the game and collect tickets for whatever prizes you want to take home.

4 Trampolines

Jump and play on our trampolines! We have 4 connected trampolines that will provide hours of delight for children of all ages.

High-speed Wi-Fi

You're welcome to join your children inside the play area, let them roam free, or relax in our comfortable seating area with complimentary Wi-Fi.
