Groups & field trips

Bring the whole class, daycare or team

At 28,000 sq ft, Allstars has the room for larger groups to spread out — with a dedicated toddler area for younger classes, an arcade and climbing structure for older kids, and Café on site for lunch.

Good fit for

Schools, daycares, preschools, summer camps, community groups and corporate family days looking for an indoor option in Edmonton, especially during winter months.

How to book

Group visits and field trips are arranged directly with our team. Call (780) 757-0055 or email info@allstarsplayland.com with your group size, preferred date, and age range so we can help plan the visit.

Group inquiries

Group Inquiries

What is considered a group?

A group is considered an organized organization or team, such as a sports team, dayhome/daycare, school, church, community organization, or other established group.

Groups may be required to provide information such as a business licence number, organization/registration number, or other documentation to verify their group status.

A group booking does not apply simply because multiple individuals are attending together. Informal groups of friends, families, or individuals who are not part of an established organization or team may be considered as general admission.

Can my group come at the last minute?

Unfortunately, we do not accept last-minute group bookings. Group bookings must be arranged and confirmed in advance.

If your group arrives without an official group booking, admission will be charged at the regular general admission rate.

Can I order individual items for my group at the cafe?

No. For group bookings, we do not allow individual kitchen items for the children. We do offer the French Fry Platter, Chicken Tender Platter, Popcorn Chicken Platter, or pizzas. Please view our online menu for pricing.

Can we bring in our own lunch boxes?

No. As per our facility policy, we do not allow any outdoor food, drinks or snacks.

Can we bring in water bottles?

Yes. Water bottles are permitted, and can be refilled at the cafe.

Do you offer group lunch?

Yes! Our group lunch option is available for an additional $6.00 + GST per child and includes:

  • Unlimited drinks during your stay
  • A designated room for your group to enjoy lunch (the room is not available for the duration of your visit)
  • One slice of cheese or pepperoni pizza per child

For group lunch, we will take a $50-$100 non-refundable deposit. Please note pricing is subject to change.

What are your group rates?
  • Monday-Thursday: $6.00 + GST/child
  • Friday-Sunday: $10.00 +GST/child

Please note pricing is subject to change.

Do I need to pay the group rate for my volunteers/supervisors who are not 18?

Yes. Volunteers and supervisors who are under 18 years old are still considered minors and must be included in the group rate, even if they are attending in a volunteer or supervisory role.

What if a child has a severe allergy/dietary restriction?

For any allergy/dietary restriction concerns, please contact us at 780-757-0055 or email us at info@allstarsplayland.com to speak to the management team.

How does payment work?

Upon arrival, we will first confirm that all required group waivers have been completed. We will then confirm the final number of children attending.

Once the attendance has been confirmed, payment will be collected in full at reception before the gates are opened.

If I order food, can I change it the day of?

Unfortunately, food orders cannot be changed on the day of your visit, as our kitchen team prepares the food in advance that morning.

If you need to make a change to your food order, please notify us at least 48 hours in advance.

Can members from my group stay later after our group leaves?

No. Once the group’s scheduled time has ended, any members who wish to stay longer will need to purchase admission at the regular general admission rate. We require everyone from the group to arrive and depart at the same time.

Can members of my group arrive at different times?

No. We require members/children to arrive and depart at the same time.

Can you keep track of the amount of kids coming from our group?

We can certainly try, however if booking with more than 30 children it can be difficult to ensure accurate numbers. It is the group organizer’s responsibility to ensure that the number of children attending matches the number included in the booking. We strongly recommend doing a head count before leaving your organization.

If the number of children has changed, please let reception know before payment is processed so we can update the total accordingly.
