FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How big is Allstars Indoor Playland?

Allstars is Edmonton's largest indoor playground at roughly 28,000 sq ft, including a climbing structure, zipline, trampolines, arcade and a dedicated toddler area.

What ages is Allstars suitable for?

All ages, from toddlers to pre-teens. There's a dedicated toddler area for ages 1–3, plus a full climbing structure, zipline and trampolines for ages 4 and up.

Is there a separate area for toddlers?

Yes — Allstars has the largest dedicated toddler play area in Edmonton, kept separate from the main climbing structure so younger kids have their own space.

How long is the zipline?

115 feet — the longest indoor zipline in Edmonton, running across the play floor.

What are your hours?

Allstars is open daily from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Hours can change on statutory holidays or during scheduled renovations — call (780) 757-0055 to confirm before visiting.

How much does admission cost?

Approximate rates are $11.95 (ages 4–17) and $8.95 (ages 1–3) on weekdays, and $16.95 / $11.95 on weekends. Adults and children under 12 months enter free. Confirm current pricing by phone.

Do you require a deposit for birthday parties?

Yes — a 50% deposit is required to secure a party booking, due within 24 hours of booking.

Can we bring outside food?

No outside food or drink is permitted, except a special cake or dessert with any party package. Allstars is a nut-free facility.

Is Allstars nut-free?

Yes, the entire facility including Café is a nut-free environment.

Where is Allstars Indoor Playland located?

Allstars is located at 9510 – 12 Ave SW, Edmonton, AB T6X 0J5, in Southwest Edmonton, with both street and private lot parking.

Do you host school or daycare field trips?

Yes — Allstars welcomes school, daycare and community group bookings. Call or email ahead to arrange group visits.
